GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered plainly.

Clear expectations about what this prototype demonstrates today.

What is MRI-RadAI?⌄

MRI-RadAI is a front-end prototype for a prostate MRI review workflow. It demonstrates a worklist, sample cases, interactive 2D and 3D views, radiologist feedback, and report exports.

Can I analyze a real MRI scan here?⌄

The research workspace accepts 1–3 PNG or JPEG images and sends them to a configured NVIDIA vision model. This is a limited image description flow, not a complete DICOM study analysis or diagnostic model.

Are the images and measurements from real scans?⌄

No. The viewer draws procedural example graphics and displays bundled sample case values. They are for product demonstration only, not clinical interpretation.

Does this connect to PACS or NVIDIA?⌄

The local research workspace can call NVIDIA's hosted vision API after you configure an API key. PACS, DICOMweb, authentication, and clinical system integrations are not included.

What does the research dataset contain?⌄

The small Task05 dataset used for the baseline contains T2 and ADC prostate MRI volumes with peripheral-zone and transition-zone segmentation labels. It does not contain cancer-lesion labels. It is separate from the synthetic graphics shown in this landing-page preview.

What will the nnU-Net model output?⌄

The planned nnU-Net v2 3D full-resolution model segments prostate anatomy into peripheral and transition zones. It will not detect cancer or lesions. The current image-upload endpoint does not serve this segmentation model.

What is planned for a working MVP?⌄

The build brief prioritizes a local backend for image upload, slice preparation, analysis status, structured findings, and clinician feedback. 3D, PACS, authentication, and PDF reporting are later phases.

Can this be used for patient care?⌄

No. This prototype is not a diagnostic device and its sample content must not be used for patient care. Clinical decisions require qualified review of original imaging and patient context.
